// about

Two decades of shipping systems that move numbers.

I've spent my career at the intersection of data, software and revenue. Today I run MiamiTake.com — a data-driven South Florida travel publication where I own the technical and analytics side of the business end to end, from the infrastructure and reporting to the paid-acquisition funnels and monetization. I co-founded the media company behind it, which builds and operates a portfolio of regional travel brands.

Earlier in my career I served as a lead software developer at Oak Ridge Associated Universities (ORAU), where I built and maintained the Veterans Health Administration's Performance Improvement Management System (PIMS) — a web-based platform that helps the VHA plan, run and evaluate emergency-preparedness exercises and manage volunteer medical personnel for deployment across more than 1,700 VA sites of care. Before that, I was lead developer at TheCoinVault.com and operations manager at Shopping Services LLC in Dandridge, Tennessee.

Across all of it, the throughline is the same: find the signal in the data, build the tool or funnel that acts on it, and hold it accountable to a number. I care about margin, measurement, and systems that keep working after I step away.

I'm a Cocke County, Tennessee native and a graduate of East Tennessee State University.
